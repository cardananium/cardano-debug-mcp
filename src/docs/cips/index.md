# Cardano standards (CIPs) cheat sheet

Short facts from the CIPs for reading what a transaction, a datum, a metadata entry, a wallet answer or a trace holds. Each section names its CIPs and their Status: Proposed means not live, do not assume it, check the protocol version and the validation context. The CIP itself (cips.cardano.org) decides where a section is silent.
